SCOPE AND LIMITS
Blocking prevents new contact/trades in both directions; it retains old messages
and completed-trade review eligibility. Suspension restricts the app and data,
not the Firebase Auth account itself. Existing listings must be reviewed/removed
separately. Moderators retain campus-limited photo evidence access. Data already
viewed or downloaded cannot be recalled.

Hosted browser tests used synthetic seller/buyer accounts and the owner account.
Concurrency limits, cross-campus/forged role cases and private message-evidence
validation were exercised in local automated tests; do not label all of those
negative cases as separate hosted browser probes.
