QuadMart — implementation facts for the policy review
Reviewed November 2, 2026

This is an internal engineering inventory, not Terms or a Privacy Policy.
It describes the development code reviewed today, not an assertion about the
older production app or every provider setting. The team must approve actual
purposes, retention periods, operator/contact details and public wording.

ACCOUNTS AND PROFILES
Firebase Authentication holds login identity and verification state. The app
does not store plaintext passwords in Firestore. Private users/<uid> records
contain email, campus, display name, counts and saved listing IDs. The public
profile contains display name, campus and reputation/trade information, not
the private email. Access remains subject to campus and account rules.
Current eligibility checks establish supported university mailbox access.
They do not establish identity or current enrollment. The separately prepared
beta gate requires server-owned approval for the Firebase UID when enabled.
betaAccess/<uid> holds only active and campusId; the owner can read their own
status. Clients cannot approve accounts or enumerate this collection. Keep the
private tester roster, emails and approval notes outside these readable records.
Membership records are not automatically deleted by account closure; the
independent lifecycle marker still denies the closed UID. Re-creating a login
with the same email creates a different UID and does not inherit admission.

On closure: the private profile/saved items are removed in resumable batches;
the login is deleted after database cleanup. Public profile identity is reduced
to “Deleted student,” UID, campus and reputation/trade aggregates. A permanent
private lifecycle marker prevents old sessions from restoring the account.
Decisions: eligible users/minimum age, re-verification, invitation approval,
profile visibility and how retained identifiers/aggregates are handled.

LISTINGS AND PHOTOS
Listings hold the seller/campus, description, price, condition and managed photo
references. Server upload accepts bounded static JPG/PNG/WebP, decodes/re-encodes
to JPEG and strips image metadata. Photos themselves may still show personal
information; re-encoding does not redact faces, addresses or belongings.
Cloudinary stores authenticated images in the restricted development namespace.
The app checks account/campus access before photo delivery.

On closure: listings are removed in batches. Unreferenced registered photos at
least 24 hours old can be deleted by maintenance; referenced, blocked or invalid
entries are held. Removed-listing archives and report snapshots preserve photo
references even after report resolution. Provider deletion must be confirmed;
the registry retains a minimal deletion marker. Twenty-four hours is a cleaner
eligibility/grace period, not a guaranteed deletion deadline. Copies already
downloaded by a viewer cannot be recalled.
Decisions: listing retention, moderation evidence retention, exception handling
and accurate deletion expectations.

MESSAGES, SALES AND REVIEWS
Conversation records include participants, display names, listing context and
the latest message; message records contain sender UID, text and time. Trade
records contain participants, listing title/price and time. Reviews link to a
completed trade and store reviewer/target IDs, score and comment. Notifications
can include a short message excerpt or trade context.

On closure: own notifications are removed, and the participant display name in
conversations becomes “Deleted student.” Shared message text, trade records,
reviews and reputation remain. Free text may contain personal information and
is not automatically anonymized by replacing the profile name. Counterparty
notifications and shared records are not all erased by account closure.
Decisions: retention periods and treatment of shared content/deletion requests.
Do not claim that closing an account deletes every piece of personal data.

REPORTS, BLOCKS AND MODERATION
Reports store the reporter/target, reason/details, listing or profile snapshot,
and, when attached to a conversation, its last ten messages. Moderator decisions
record actor, action, explanation, version and time. Removed listings are kept
in a private archive; account restriction state is separate from login identity.
Reporters see a limited outcome, not moderator evidence or other reporters.

On closure: own block records are removed. Incoming blocks, reports, snapshots,
moderation events/archives and account lifecycle/deletion markers remain. Cleanup
does not silently erase this evidence. Role assignment is private and managed
outside the public app; the team still needs an access/coverage/appeals owner.
Decisions: allowed uses, authorized reviewers, retention and appeal handling.

ABUSE CONTROLS AND LOGS
Private quotas, request ledgers and media digests support limits and retry safety.
The application log projection emits operation/route, request reference, status,
duration and a bounded failure code; it excludes request bodies, identities,
tokens and arbitrary exceptions. Vercel, Firebase/reCAPTCHA and Cloudinary may
have separate service/access logs; this application projection does not govern
their content or retention. Provider settings require their own review.
Decisions: monitoring owners, access, review cadence and log/ledger retention.

BACKUPS AND RECOVERY
Complete encrypted backups include database records, Firebase Auth recovery
material (including password hashes) and photo bytes. Private local storage and
the approved owner-only Google Drive folder hold encrypted archives. The key is
kept separately by the owner; it does not belong in chat, source or a backup.
An uploaded file counts as verified only after download, checksum/decryption and
owner-only receipt checks. Isolated restore accounts are disabled/quarantined.

Deleting a live record does not rewrite older encrypted backups. The repository
local pruning helper defaults to retaining 28 archives; this is not an offsite
retention promise. The approved recurring Drive workflow does not delete Drive
files and stops at its 1 GiB budget. The scheduled pinned runtime and actual
backup inventory must be reviewed before promising a retention deadline.
Decisions: approved archive lifetime, how closure records/deletion requests are
reconciled after restoration, recovery ownership and missed-run coverage.
Do not release recovered accounts/photos until those checks have passed.

PROVIDER AND BROWSER REVIEW BEFORE PUBLICATION
Known development services: Firebase Auth/Firestore/App Check with reCAPTCHA
Enterprise, Vercel hosting, Cloudinary photos, and encrypted Google Drive backup.
Confirm the actual operator, service settings, locations, access permissions,
browser/session storage and provider notices for the chosen beta deployment.
This inventory is not an exhaustive provider data-processing or legal review.
Do not describe university-mailbox checks as university endorsement.
