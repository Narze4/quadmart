IMPLEMENTED
- Recent-password confirmation starts a self-only, resumable deletion job.
- A private closure marker immediately blocks marketplace access and contact.
- Bounded, checkpointed batches remove listings, private profile data, own
  notifications and block records, and replace the public/conversation name.
- Login deletion occurs only after database cleanup. Interrupted jobs resume;
  a guarded operator command can reconcile failed or out-of-band Auth deletion.

  remain. Minimal public reputation and private lifecycle tombstones remain.

  development paths plus live listings, archived listings and report snapshots.
- Provider deletion is claimed transactionally, retries safely and cannot race
  successfully with new photo attachment or late upload finalization.
- No runtime Auth administrator role, production deployment or scheduler added.

LIMITS / BEFORE INVITING STUDENTS
Maintenance is currently an operator workflow, not a scheduled service. Jobs,
orphaned data and unused photos can remain indefinitely without it. A normal
browser deletion leaves its job at the Auth checkpoint until reconciliation.
Choose and implement scheduling, stale-job visibility, alerts and operational
ownership. Agree on retention/erasure policy for shared history, evidence and
tombstones; this implementation does not delete every piece of personal data.
Complete remaining pagination, backup/restore rehearsal, edge abuse controls,
support/moderation policy and a final phone/multiple-account rehearsal.
