QUADMART SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE AND FIRESTORE RECOVERY - OCTOBER 28, 2026

IMPLEMENTATION
- A private pinned local runtime runs bounded development maintenance.
- A fresh encrypted Firestore backup must be written/decrypted/verified first.
- A 15-minute lease prevents overlap; saved cursors resume bounded scans.
- Missing Auth reconciliation requires positive absence; active moderators and
  malformed profiles require review. Other account/photo safeguards are retained.
- Reports contain safe counts/phases. Failed runs exit unsuccessfully; launch
  reports also cover configuration/dependency errors and eight-minute timeouts.
- A six-hour Codex heartbeat is configured for this Mac. The user chose to keep
  the free development setup; no billing, production or runtime IAM was changed.
- A weekly local restore launcher starts its own empty isolated emulator and
  checks the newest encrypted backup. It refuses stale backups and cloud targets.

LIMITS
- Scheduling requires this Mac, network access and the desktop app. A sleeping
  or unavailable Mac cannot deliver a missed-run alert until it resumes.
- Local credentials and task permissions must remain available. A denied
  permission/expired login is a failure to report, not a reason to bypass checks.
- The encrypted archive covers Firestore documents plus source configuration;
  it does not include Firebase Auth passwords/accounts or Cloudinary image bytes.
- Key and archives reside on one Mac. Loss of that Mac is not covered without
  an independently protected off-device copy and separate recovery-key custody.
- Restores are deliberately restricted to a disposable local emulator. Cloud
  disaster recovery, Auth/photo recovery, retention policy and reconciliation of
  later account closures/moderation actions remain pre-launch work.
- No claim of production readiness follows from this rehearsal.
