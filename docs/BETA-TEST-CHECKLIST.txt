BEFORE THE FIRST STUDENT INVITATIONS — IN ORDER

[ ] 1. Finalize the beta scope and eligibility. Owner: founding team.
    Confirmed November 2: 5–10 Emory students, fresh test data, quadmart-beta.
    Still name the
    exact test site and dates. Define current students versus
    alumni/staff and how eligibility will be checked again. Decide whether beta
    data will be retained or reset and tell testers. University-mailbox access
    does not itself prove current enrollment. No invitations have been sent.

[ ] 2. Complete a genuinely fresh signup. Owner: a tester + engineering.
    Use an unused university email the tester controls; preserve the owner's
    existing account and do not assume an email alias works. On the chosen host,
    sign up under enforced App Check, open the actual email, verify and continue.
    Pass: marketplace blocked before verification; correct campus/profile and
    dashboard afterward; reload works; duplicate-account guidance is safe.

[ ] 3. Complete the hosted password-reset flow. Owner: account holder.
    This remains deliberately deferred. The holder requests/opens the actual
    email, chooses the password privately, then signs in. Pass: new password
    works, old password fails and the used link cannot be reused. Local reset
    tests are already green; they do not replace this hosted check.

[ ] 4. Test real iPhone/Safari and Android/Chrome. Owner: two device testers.
    Follow PHONE-ACCEPTANCE-CHECKLIST.txt: camera/gallery and HEIC, permissions,
    uploads/photos, keyboard, touch targets, scrolling, rotation, slow/cellular
    network and offline retry. Background the app for more than 65 minutes,
    resume and confirm access/renewal without losing drafts. A simulated mobile
    browser or frozen desktop renderer does not complete this step.

[ ] 5. Assign support and moderation. Owner: founding team.
    Choose a named primary and backup, coverage hours, escalation/appeals owner
    and realistic response expectations. Verify hello@quadmart.app is owned and
    monitored through an owner-directed receive/reply check. Agree prohibited
    listings and report handling; rehearse report/block/suspend/restore with
    disposable accounts. Do not promise continuous coverage unless staffed.

[ ] 6. Approve and publish the user policies. Owner: founding team/reviewer.
    Finalize Terms, Privacy, eligibility, listing rules and retention/deletion
    treatment for messages, reviews, moderation records and older backups.
    Provide accurate operator/contact details and review the wording before
    making the currently unavailable Terms/Privacy links live. These policies
    have not been invented or treated as approved by engineering.

[ ] 7. Configure and rehearse the exact beta destination. Owner: engineering + team.
    Complete the separate quadmart-beta environment; preserve development data.
    Apply/retest Auth redirect domains, App Check, API firewall and access rules
    for its actual hostname; current coverage is the protected development
    preview. Test the invitation/access path without disabling protection on
    existing development data. Verify rules/indexes and environment isolation.
    Review Firestore App Check monitoring before any enforcement decision.
    The owner chose fresh data; no production/development migration or wipe is
    authorized. Verify an uninvited verified Emory user is denied by the server,
    direct Firestore rules and protected photos, and cannot approve themselves.
    Review rollback and cloud restore/cutover in an approved target.

[ ] 8. Confirm operational coverage. Owner: named operator + backup.
    Scheduled-cycle verification is complete (see DONE above). Check backup age,
    owner-only receipt, restore evidence, storage and free-tier usage at go/no-go.
    Agree acceptable data loss/recovery time and a missed-run response. Keep the
    Mac online/available and the app running if retaining this free setup; it
    cannot independently alert while unavailable. Continuous availability and
    named coverage are not established by two successful scheduled runs.

[ ] 9. Run the final two-person beta acceptance test. Owner: seller + buyer.
    On the chosen hostname: sign in, publish a real test photo/listing, find and
    save it, message, reserve, complete the seller-selected sale, check both
    receipts/notifications, leave reviews and update profiles. Also verify
    failed-request retries, blocking/reporting and sign-out. Record browser,
    device, build, result and safe error reference; never capture passwords or
    tokens. Require green CI and the correct Ready deployment for that build.
    QuadMart records a trade; it does not process payment or prove handoff.

[ ] 10. Record the team's go/no-go, then invite the chosen group. Owner: team.
    Assign a feedback/bug triage owner and agree what stops the beta: access to
    another user's private data, lost/duplicate sales, broad signup/login failure,
    unsafe moderation behavior or unresolved recovery failure. Stop expansion
    when these occur, preserve evidence and retest the fix before resuming.
